# Separate multi-turn development pilot

The [S15 `REASON-015` scoring-dimension probe](scoring_dimension_probe_reason015_s15_v1.json) is a frozen one-sentence deletion from the existing batch-G strong answer, not a new family. Its EN/ZH exact-answer hashes are verified by `python3 -m benchmark.scoring_dimension_hypothesis` alongside saved S9/S11/S14 rating comparisons. The subsequent [S16 eight-call judge-only result](../../docs/S16_REASON015_JUDGE_RESULT_2026-10-21_EN.md) is development evidence, not a new case, validated label or calibrated score. Future published-case intake should follow the [PubMed/PMC source policy](../../docs/PUBMED_CASE_SOURCE_POLICY_2026-10-21_EN.md) and [citation-only pilot](../../docs/S16_PUBMED_INTAKE_2026-10-21_EN.md); finding a PubMed abstract does not license copying a patient narrative or validate an answer label.

The [S17 v2 clean-punctuation probe](scoring_dimension_probe_reason015_s17_v2.json) was committed before a second eight-call judge-only result; see the [English readout](../../docs/S17_CLEAN_PROBE_RESULT_2026-10-21_EN.md). It is a separate public-development artifact, not a new family or scoring key. The [historical S17 PubMed source-to-answer screen](../../docs/S17_PUBMED_SOURCE_TO_ANSWER_2026-10-21_EN.md) preceded the later batch-H drafts.

An [S18 exact-packet replay](../../docs/S18_JUDGE_REPEATABILITY_RESULT_2026-10-21_EN.md) found that two of four omitted-safety-net scores shifted on repeat, although all four baseline scores stayed at 100. The repeat is a judge diagnostic only; no family, anchor, rubric or answer key was promoted.

[S19](../../docs/S19_ANCHOR_AND_MULTIFAMILY_PLAN_2026-10-21_EN.md) proposes a non-enforced boundary and a multi-family repeatability plan; no new judge call has occurred.

The later [S19 result](../../docs/S19_MULTIFAMILY_REPEATABILITY_RESULT_2026-10-21_EN.md) completed 24 judge-only repeat calls on `TRIAGE-018/019`: 15/24 numeric scores and 22/24 provisional pass flags repeated, although all criterion-status and critical-error vectors matched. The line above records the pre-run state, not the latest result. No scoring rule or case label was promoted.

`pilot_cases.json` contains the original four synthetic two-turn families; `batch_c_cases.json` adds 12, `batch_d_cases.json` adds four, `batch_e_cases.json` adds ten, `batch_f_cases.json` adds four source-linked reasoning probes, `batch_g_cases.json` adds four low-acuity/de-escalation controls, `batch_h_cases.json` adds two PubMed-informed original synthetic cases, and `batch_i_cases.json` adds one steroid-safety draft. The configured public multi-turn development track has **41 families / 82 English/Chinese conversations**, all draft and unreviewed. The scripted second user message is withheld until the candidate answers the first. This track is **not** part of the 32-family/64-prompt single-turn Alpha, and its scores must not be pooled into that Alpha average. New prospective calibration drafts live in [`../calibration/`](../calibration/), outside the default runner; hidden draft material is local-only, not in Git.

Each family names a transition failure mode and public sources. Each turn has candidate-visible `user` text and private draft `critical_requirements`/`critical_errors`. The two languages share error IDs and severities, but semantic equivalence has not been human reviewed. The [batch-F provenance ledger](batch_f_provenance.json) records published-case mechanisms and source-to-label limits; three of its four families are case-report-informed. The [batch-G ledger](batch_g_provenance.json) marks all four controls as guidance-derived synthetic cases, not published patient adaptations. The [batch-H ledger](batch_h_provenance.json) and [batch-I ledger](batch_i_provenance.json) separate PubMed mechanism inspiration from independent guidance for all 18 draft requirements, with privacy/leakage and unresolved-consent notes; they are structurally checked by `python3 -m benchmark.pubmed_derived_case_audit`, not clinically validated. `contrast_transcripts.json` holds six English-only safe/unsafe pairs plus five safe/partial/unsafe triplets for judge diagnostics, not Gold labels. The two newest pairs have not produced a live judge result.

Validate the combined contract with `python3 -m benchmark.cli validate-multiturn`. The [pilot report](../../docs/MULTITURN_PILOT_2026-10-11_EN.md) documents the local workflow and old smoke results; [batch C](../../docs/MULTITURN_BATCH_C_2026-10-11_EN.md), [batch D](../../docs/MULTITURN_BATCH_D_2026-10-12_EN.md), and [batch E](../../docs/BATCH_E_100_FAMILIES_2026-10-14_EN.md) map added probes and review questions. Do not add real patient information, use these draft labels as clinical advice, or call a passing infrastructure test clinical validation.
