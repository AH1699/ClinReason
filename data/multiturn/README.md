# Separate multi-turn development pilot

`pilot_cases.json` contains the original four synthetic two-turn families; `batch_c_cases.json` adds 12, `batch_d_cases.json` adds four, `batch_e_cases.json` adds ten, `batch_f_cases.json` adds four source-linked reasoning probes, and `batch_g_cases.json` adds four low-acuity/de-escalation controls. The configured public multi-turn development track has **38 families / 76 English/Chinese conversations**, all draft and unreviewed. The scripted second user message is withheld until the candidate answers the first. This track is **not** part of the 32-family/64-prompt single-turn Alpha, and its scores must not be pooled into that Alpha average. New prospective calibration drafts live in [`../calibration/`](../calibration/), outside the default runner; hidden draft material is local-only, not in Git.

Each family names a transition failure mode and public sources. Each turn has candidate-visible `user` text and private draft `critical_requirements`/`critical_errors`. The two languages share error IDs and severities, but semantic equivalence has not been human reviewed. The [batch-F provenance ledger](batch_f_provenance.json) records published-case mechanisms and source-to-label limits; three of its four families are case-report-informed. The [batch-G ledger](batch_g_provenance.json) marks all four new controls as guidance-derived synthetic cases, not published patient adaptations. `contrast_transcripts.json` holds six English-only safe/unsafe pairs plus five safe/partial/unsafe triplets for judge diagnostics, not Gold labels. The two newest pairs have not produced a live judge result.

Validate the combined contract with `python3 -m benchmark.cli validate-multiturn`. The [pilot report](../../docs/MULTITURN_PILOT_2026-10-11_EN.md) documents the local workflow and old smoke results; [batch C](../../docs/MULTITURN_BATCH_C_2026-10-11_EN.md), [batch D](../../docs/MULTITURN_BATCH_D_2026-10-12_EN.md), and [batch E](../../docs/BATCH_E_100_FAMILIES_2026-10-14_EN.md) map added probes and review questions. Do not add real patient information, use these draft labels as clinical advice, or call a passing infrastructure test clinical validation.
