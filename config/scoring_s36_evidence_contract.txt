Shared evidence contract for EVERY response_evidence field: supply one contiguous exact span from one allowed assistant turn, or use the applicable empty-evidence exception. Never stitch passages, insert ellipses (including ... or ……), slashes, quotation wrappers, connecting words or paraphrases absent from the original span. Literal punctuation already in the span is allowed. Existing Unicode NFKC, Markdown-marker and whitespace normalization is unchanged; it does not permit semantic paraphrase. Explain other passages in rationale or observation explanations, not inside a fabricated quotation.

For criterion_assessments[].response_evidence, the allowed turn is only the matching-stage assistant answer. Met and partial require nonempty evidence, except met avoidance anchors starting exactly 'Does not ' or 'Avoids '. Missed or unclear can use an empty quote with rationale explaining the absence. Prior-turn facts may inform context, but cannot replace a missing update action.

For update dimension_observations[].response_evidence, either visible assistant turn is allowed, but a single quote cannot combine them. Empty evidence is allowed when no behavior is quoted. This does not waive overlap_note, the revision observation's new_fact, or the distinct_failure explanation. User text is never assistant-response evidence. Initial judgments have no dimension_observations. Critical-error rationale and explanatory fields are prose, not additional quote-bearing fields.
