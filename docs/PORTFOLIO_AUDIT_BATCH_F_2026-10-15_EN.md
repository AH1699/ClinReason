## What changed

They are different tasks and must not be pooled into a clinical score. All remain draft and unreviewed; professional scoring remains disabled. They add chronic GI medication review, perioperative differential reasoning, pediatric evolution, and neuromuscular ventilation risk. Their prompts and labels are new synthetic drafts, not patient records or article text. The [adaptation registry](../data/evidence/clinical_case_adaptations.json) records article attribution, license, changes and limitations for all **26** published-case adaptations. A case report supports a possible pattern; it does not prove our rubric, frequency, or severity.

## Mechanism and evidence audit

Run `python3 -m benchmark.portfolio_audit` for the machine-readable snapshot. The [author-triage queue](../data/audit/overlap_and_source_review.json) records **nine selected overlap pairs** across all three tracks. Eight should be kept in one group in any prospective split; one negative-celiac-test pair is explicitly *not* grouped because it probes different mechanisms. The closest potential construct duplicate is `HC-MT-TRIAGE-001` versus `HC-MT-TRIAGE-008`: both change from mild initial advice to emergency breathing symptoms. Allergy versus respiratory infection may separate them, but that has not been demonstrated by candidate responses. Do not count them as two independent difficulty observations until reviewed. Cross-track repetitions include duplicate acetaminophen ingredients, CKD chronicity, anticoagulant-plus-NSAID advice, medication-list reconciliation, lithium risk, and possible PE. Different persona or turn structure can be valuable, but needs a demonstrated distinct failure mode.

The audit confirms that every Alpha family has a matching family-level public-source registry URL and all 26 professional published-case families have adaptation records. It also finds **zero criterion-level *verified* source-to-label mappings**: source URLs and general “supports/limit” notes do not independently validate each reference element or critical-error severity. This applies to all 104 draft families; it is a review task, not evidence that the clinical labels are wrong. The queue prioritizes eight source-to-label checks, seven high priority, especially rare false-negative examples and asymptomatic blood-pressure or stable medication inquiries. **53/104** families contain a severity-3 critical error; its individual appropriateness remains uncalibrated. Common guidance URLs recur in 12 URL clusters, but shared guidance is not itself a duplicate case.

## Split and difficulty plan

The [prospective split plan](../data/audit/prospective_split_plan.json) keeps **all 104 public families in development**, with **zero calibration** and **zero hidden Gold** families. For a first new, privately managed tranche, it proposes *targets*, not power or adequacy claims: 10 calibration and 20 hidden families per track (30 and 60 total). They must be newly authored after split registration, reviewed independently, and balanced by workflow, acuity (including nonemergency controls), specialty, care setting, mechanism, language and source cluster. English/Chinese versions and paraphrases stay in the same split. A case adapted from a public report has model-memorization risk; merely withholding our rewrite does not eliminate it. Hidden prompts and keys should not be committed to this repository. Professional documentation slots need a separate validated contract rather than being silently filled with more consults. A statistician must set uncertainty and minimum-sample rules before per-category rankings.

The first controlled overlap probe now has a safe, partial and unsafe English update for `HC-MT-TRIAGE-008`. The partial answer escalates correctly but omits the prescribed auto-injector condition that distinguishes the allergy item from generic respiratory escalation. Its case binding and expected ordering pass an offline fixture. An opt-in Gemini judge attempt for the three responses failed at transport with unknown delivery and wrote no result; it was not retried or counted. Next non-clinical work: complete this judge probe when transport is reliable, test a few other mechanism groups, then run a bounded, blinded multi-candidate diagnostic on frozen development items with independent judging. That can reveal ceiling effects and candidate/judge inconsistency but **cannot** turn provisional labels into clinical truth. Qualified clinical and bilingual review is deferred for now, not waived before release. In particular, the Kawasaki, porphyria, PE and GCA source-to-label entries need specialty review before any professional claim.

## Verification boundary

The portfolio audit checks IDs, family counts, declared source binding, selected overlap groups, and split-plan consistency. Dataset validators check structure and bilingual critical-error IDs/severities. Neither check proves clinical correctness, Chinese equivalence, source currency, real-world difficulty, or model ranking. No live model score was produced in this batch.
