# S19 scoring-boundary proposal and multi-family repeatability plan — 2026-10-21

The [S18 same-packet replay](S18_JUDGE_REPEATABILITY_RESULT_2026-10-21_EN.md) found a `partial→unclear` change for the same Chinese `REASON-015` omission answer. The [S19 proposed boundary](../config/reason015_anchor_boundary_s19_v1.json) makes the ambiguity testable **without changing the enforced batch-G anchor**: present absence of symptoms, pregnancy or a planned procedure is not prospective safety advice. Under this author hypothesis, both future new-symptom reassessment *and* future pregnancy/relevant-procedure exception language are `met`; exactly one is `partial`; neither is `missed`; `unclear` is reserved for genuinely ambiguous or incomplete answer evidence and triggers human adjudication rather than automatic midpoint credit. Thus the frozen S17/S18 one-sentence deletion is *provisionally hypothesized* as `missed`, not retrospectively relabeled. Clinical, bilingual and methods review (H82) may reject or modify this boundary.

The proposal separates three reasons for deductions. A missing answer element affects requirement completeness. A direct-safety deduction needs a separately described risk caused by the answer, not merely the same omitted sentence. A revision deduction needs evidence that the second-turn facts were not incorporated; retaining the clarified current negatives and revising the treatment decision may support good revision even with a weak future safety net. The existing named critical error is over-treatment from the abnormal culture alone, not omission of prospective advice. None of this changes the numeric rubric, threshold, judge prompt, prior judgments or the aspirational ~60–70/~80/~90 model bands.

Any live repeat requires a fresh ignored `work/` directory, `--max-api-calls 24`, zero candidate calls and no automatic retries: one exact repeat per cell. The program writes a plan before the first call, stops on failure without a completion record, and refuses to resume an existing output directory. Its `audit` compares score/status/dimension/critical-error/pass/review agreement separately by family, language, answer type and judge. Provider-default sampling and possible service drift remain limitations. This is a *protocol*, not a completed repeatability finding or a score-contract decision. No API calls were made for S19.
