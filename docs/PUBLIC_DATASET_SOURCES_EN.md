# Public data/source options for case expansion — checked 2026-10-11

The [catalog the user identified](https://www.shujuyoupu.com/datasets/catalog/) describes eight evaluation products, including longitudinal reasoning, retrieval, safety, privacy, and independent validation. Its product volumes and prices are negotiated by project. The linked evaluation-category page could not be retrieved during this check. Public sample visibility is **not** permission to copy, redistribute, or use underlying de-identified records; we have not ingested any of its samples.

| Source | What is available | Fit and decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Synthea sample downloads](https://synthetichealth.github.io/downloads.html) | Synthetic patient histories in multiple formats; the project says these data are free of cost, privacy, and security restrictions. | **Best next input source** for longitudinal, multi-event *synthetic* cases. Still requires authored questions, source-linked expected behavior, clinician review, and a license/provenance check for the exact exported file. No Synthea records are in Alpha yet. |

Not expert answer labels. |

Public guidance is not a validated case label, and a current source page is not a globally universal treatment pathway. |

## Ingestion rule

Before considering an external dataset: record exact version/URL, license covering the **data** (not just code), attribution/redistribution obligations, patient-data provenance, access conditions, train/test leakage risk, target population and jurisdiction, and whether expert labels exist. Keep externally derived cases in a separate, provenance-tagged split. Do not turn a free sample of real patient data into our public Gold Set by default.

The first six new Alpha families are documented in [batch A](LABEL_EXPANSION_2026-10-11_EN.md); 12 further original synthetic families are documented in [batch B](LABEL_EXPANSION_BATCH_B_2026-10-11_EN.md). They are informed by the public guidance in [`public_sources.json`](../data/evidence/public_sources.json); no external cases or patient records were copied. They are **not** clinically or bilingually validated.
