## What changed in scoring work

The versioned [S20 review-only rule](../config/paired_judge_evidence_s20_v1.json) and [offline audit](../benchmark/paired_judge_evidence_s20.py) re-verify the exact S19 saved packets, raw judgments and scoring formula, then queue dimension changes and provisional pass flips. They make **zero candidate/judge calls** and do **not** change the judge prompt, criterion anchors, weights, threshold, historical scores, or a candidate's official result. The 60–70 / ~80 / ~90 score bands remain an unfit aspiration, not tuning targets.

Across 24 exact-packet source/repeat pairs, 9 had unchanged criterion-status and named-error vectors but a changed requirements/safety/revision rating. Five pairs changed a safety rating, two a revision rating, and two a requirements rating; these categories overlap the nine but not each other in this panel. Two other pairs changed only the judge's human-review request. The **two held pass flips** are Grok's same `TRIAGE-018` partial answers in EN and ZH: each moved 65→72.5 because update safety moved 2→3 while update requirements, revision, statuses and named errors did not. Both rationales recognized absent routine follow-up and red-flag safety-net advice. Their rationales do not independently establish whether the safety rating should be 2 or 3.

The rule asks a later adjudicator to record the exact answer span or absence, case criterion/anchor, a **distinct foreseeable harm or missed escalation** for any safety deduction, and a **separate failure to use new-turn information** for any revision deduction. It also asks for source applicability, an independent clinical decision and bilingual equivalence. If the identical packet flips provisional pass, the comparison is held from any publishable model score even if the judge checked “human review.” This is a review queue, **not** an automatic new pass/fail rule. A missing requirement alone does not prove a second safety deduction or an unobserved revision failure. The S19 authored contrasts and AI explanations are not human Gold. Run `python3 -m benchmark.paired_judge_evidence_s20 --summary` only in this checkout with the intentionally ignored saved S19 `work/` files; a clean clone cannot reproduce that local audit without securely supplied raw results.

## New public-development case

[Batch I](../data/multiturn/batch_i_cases.json) adds **one bilingual two-turn medication-safety family**, `HC-MT-MEDSAFE-015`, to test re-evaluation after a previously unidentified long-term anti-inflammatory tablet is identified as an oral glucocorticoid and the patient reports repeated vomiting, inability to retain medication/fluids and near-fainting. The [PubMed case report, PMID 41458680](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41458680/), motivates the **general withdrawal/adrenal-crisis mechanism only**; the synthetic adult is not the report's topical-steroid patient and no patient details, numbers, treatment or outcome were imported. The [2024 ESE/Endocrine Society guideline](https://www.endocrine.org/clinical-practice-guidelines/glucocorticoid-induced-adrenal-insufficiency) supplies separate support for all six English draft requirements, especially considering adrenal crisis with recent glucocorticoid use plus vomiting or instability and clinician-led parenteral care when oral intake fails. The exact urgency, severity-3 error, no-dose wording and Chinese equivalence are **author proposals, not clinician-approved labels**.

The [source intake](../data/evidence/pubmed_case_intake_s20_v1.json) and [per-requirement provenance](../data/multiturn/batch_i_provenance.json) keep report and answer authority separate. The PMC article reports CC BY 4.0; the PubMed abstract states publication consent was “obtained or waived,” not a patient-specific written-consent determination. This uncertainty is explicitly allowed only for this synthetic, no-narrative public draft and remains an external-release gate; article correction/retraction, privacy, applicability and leakage review are also open. The [offline audit](../benchmark/pubmed_derived_case_audit.py) checks structural linkage and rejects copied distinctive report details, not clinical truth or proof of non-identifiability.
