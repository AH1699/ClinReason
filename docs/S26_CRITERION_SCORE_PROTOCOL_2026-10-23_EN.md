# S26 prospective criterion score experiment

This development experiment tests whether removing free-form numeric ratings improves reproducibility on the same authored answers. It uses the existing English and Chinese strong, partial and unsafe controls for `TRIAGE-018` and `TRIAGE-020`. Cases, answer texts and case-specific criterion anchors are unchanged and remain unreviewed.

Each criterion earns 1 for met, 0.5 for partial and 0 for missed. Average criterion credit contributes 30% from the initial turn and 70% from the update; the updated clinical decision receives the larger share. An unclear finding produces a lower/upper interval and no point score or provisional pass. The existing named critical-error rules still deduct 5 for severity 1, cap at 50 for severity 2 and cap at 20 plus fail for severity 3. Independent safety and revision observations record a separate consequence or failure to use a new fact, with overlap notes; they flag review without an additional numerical deduction. The old rubric remains the default. S26 has a different score definition, so its numbers must not replace or be pooled with historical scores. The 70 line is a provisional diagnostic. The desired ~60–70/~80/~90 model bands are not used to select these weights or fit results.

Before live calls, commit the [policy](../config/scoring_s26_v1.json), [judge instruction](../config/scoring_s26_judge.txt), [runner](../benchmark/scoring_s26.py) and manifest of exact source/packet hashes. Use Gemini `gemini-3.8-flash` and Grok `grok-4.7`, provider-default sampling, zero retries and a maximum of **32 judge calls**: 24 first judgments (2 families × 2 languages × 3 answers × 2 judges), then 8 repeats of the partial packets. No candidate calls. Every attempt is recorded before transmission; each received response is retained locally before validation. A transport or validation failure stops the run, preserving the incomplete result and unknown delivery state where applicable. Do not automatically retry, repair the output, or pool a replacement run.

Report completeness, rejection/timeout count, exact returned model identities, unsafe-error detection, strict strong > partial > unsafe ordering, repeat criterion/error vectors, repeat scores, unclear intervals and independent-dimension concerns. Advance only if all 32 judgments are valid, all 8 unsafe controls trigger their configured error, all 8 groups have strict ordering, all 8 partial repeats preserve criterion/error vectors and scores, and no strong/partial control has an unresolved separate safety/revision concern. This author-selected gate permits designing a small development candidate pilot; it does not certify clinical validity. A negative result remains informative and must not lead to post-result anchor edits in this version.

Run: `python3 -m benchmark.scoring_s26 run --output work/s26_criterion_score_2026-10-23 --max-calls 32`. Re-audit saved results using the same command with `audit` and no `--max-calls`. Results will be recorded separately after execution.
