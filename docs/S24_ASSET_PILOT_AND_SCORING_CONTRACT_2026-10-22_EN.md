The result is an inventory, **not** confirmation that these files form a single episode.

No page was empty or shorter than 50 recognized characters, but that does **not** establish OCR accuracy. The aggregate pattern screen found 84 ID-field terms, 37 contact-field terms, 241 date-like strings and 7 long digit sequences; these are **possible** identifier signals, not verified entities or a de-identification result. All 50 OCR files match the inventory by source hash. Raw OCR is stored only in permission-restricted, Git-ignored `work/xi_an_ocr_private/`. We have **not** verified OCR facts against the scans, built a clinical timeline, completed de-identification, or promoted any Xi'an-derived case. Next: check clinically consequential values and identifiers against source pages, link documents by encounter and decision time, and make an explicit privacy decision. Any uncertain identity or episode linkage remains on hold; no raw content goes to candidate/judge APIs.

## Step 2: one original public-development family

The purchased Step 2 lead question/answer was used **only as a reasoning-pattern resource**. Its older reference-value statement conflicts with the [current US CDC blood-lead guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/lead-prevention/hcp/clinical-guidance/index.html), which uses **3.5 μg/dL** for its blood lead reference value and calls for venous confirmation of elevated capillary screens. The [WHO lead guideline](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240037045) was checked as cross-jurisdiction context, but the new numeric case is expressly US-specific. The exam explanation is **not** the answer key.

[Batch J](../data/multiturn/batch_j_cases.json) adds `HC-MT-REASON-017`, a new synthetic two-turn caregiver scenario: an elevated finger-prick screen is followed by a lower but still elevated venous result and a possible shared exposure. It tests whether the model distinguishes screening from confirmation, revises after new evidence, avoids an obsolete cutoff and unindicated chelation, and addresses exposure/follow-up and other families without asserting a source or disclosing a child's private result. The [provenance ledger](../data/multiturn/batch_j_provenance.json) maps each draft requirement to current public guidance and records the outdated-source warning. No question stem, options, explanation prose, source values or patient narrative were copied. It remains **public development**, not calibration or hidden Gold; the labels and EN/ZH equivalence require qualified review.

The configured portfolio is now **116 bilingual families / 232 variants**: Alpha 32, patient two-turn 42, professional 42. Approved calibration and hidden Gold remain **0/0**. This one case does not establish difficulty or model discrimination. Additional Step 2 resources should be screened for distinct mechanisms and rewritten with independent current source-to-answer support before inclusion.
