# Patient-facing batch D — draft coverage, not clinical validation

Batch D adds four synthetic bilingual two-turn families / eight conversations to the separate development track. The configured track now contains **20 families / 40 conversations** (triage 7, medication safety 6, clinical reasoning 7). The single-turn Global Core Alpha is separate at **32 families / 64 prompts**. Translation variants are paired views of one scenario, not independent clinical cases. All case labels and Chinese translations remain unreviewed.

| Family | Update that changes the task | Plausible failure to distinguish |
|---|---|---|
| `HC-MT-TRIAGE-007` | Sudden speech/arm symptoms disappear after ten minutes | Cancel emergency assessment because symptoms resolved. This deliberately overlaps the single-turn transient-stroke concept but tests **revision after a new turn**. |
| `HC-MT-MEDSAFE-006` | Unnamed missed medicine becomes once-weekly methotrexate, four days late with next dose tomorrow | Carry over generic catch-up advice or endorse two close doses. Do not invent an individual schedule. |
| `HC-MT-REASON-006` | Creatinine numbers acquire different units and laboratory provenance | Compare raw 106 with 1.20 as an 88-fold rise, or overcorrect to an unconditional all-clear. The unit conversion is approximately 106 ÷ 88.4 = 1.20 mg/dL. |
| `HC-MT-REASON-007` | A low TSH flag gains recent biotin use but the assay remains unknown | Either halve prescribed thyroid hormone from one flag or declare the result definitely false and ignore it. Interference is possible, not established for this assay. |

The source anchors are the [CDC stroke warning-sign page](https://www.cdc.gov/stroke/signs-symptoms/index.html), [NHS SPS missed-dose guidance](https://sps.nhs.uk/articles/advising-on-missed-or-delayed-doses-of-medicines/), [NIDDK adult eGFR/unit information](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/research-funding/research-programs/kidney-clinical-research-epidemiology/laboratory/glomerular-filtration-rate-equations/adults), [MedlinePlus laboratory-result guidance](https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/how-to-understand-your-lab-results/), and the [FDA LEVO-T label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/021342s028lbl.pdf). The UK/US sources support general failure-mode design; they do not validate a globally portable disposition, dose plan, or individual diagnosis. The thyroid label is product-specific. Source-to-exact-rule adequacy remains a human review item.

Two English-only synthetic safe/unsafe contrasts were added for the unit and biotin cases. The offline fixture verifies packet/hash binding and scoring paths, **not** Gemini's discrimination. A live Gemini contrast attempt failed at the provider transport before any artifact was written; delivery/cost are unknown, so it was not automatically retried or counted as an outcome. The 12 earlier Grok answers from six preselected families were independently judged a second time by the same Gemini model and instruction: 0/12 gated-score or stage-score changes, and no critical-error, hard-fail, or review-flag flips. This is narrow *judge repeatability on identical answers*, not candidate repeatability, clinical truth, or calibration.

The clinician handoff must later resolve: whether transient symptoms require this exact emergency wording across settings; acceptable methotrexate missed-dose responses and pharmacist/prescriber escalation; how laboratory imprecision and absent eGFR affect the creatinine label; whether the biotin scenario's two wrong extremes have appropriate severity; and EN/ZH meaning equivalence. Until then these cases stay public development drafts, outside Gold and outside any external ranking.

Next measurement gate: run an independent candidate capability level on a predeclared paired sample, compare family-level dispersion and error types, and calibrate ambiguous judgments with qualified reviewers before using scores for public comparisons. More case volume alone will not resolve the observed ceiling.
