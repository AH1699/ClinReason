### Why Gemini's request 21 was rejected

The requested model returned successfully, but initial requirement 2 quoted two nonadjacent parts of MiniMax's Chinese answer with an inserted `……`. Both pieces are present in the answer; their joined string is not. That violates the frozen exact-span evidence rule. This is a **judge-output contract failure**, not an authentication, credit or MiniMax generation failure, and not proof that the underlying clinical answer is wrong.

The [read-only diagnostic](../benchmark/minimax_s34_diagnostic.py) reproduces the failure and records the two literal pieces in a [hash-bound report](../data/audit/s34_validation_stop_diagnostic.json). The rejected response remains unscored. The diagnostic does not remove the ellipsis, accept the pieces, infer a score or authorize resumption.
