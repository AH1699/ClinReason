## Copyable acquisition instruction for the data team

> Acquire **30 distinct, fully linkable patient episodes for a first pilot** (not 30 documents): 10 evolving triage, 10 medication-safety, and 10 diagnostic-reasoning episodes, with a mix of adult, older-adult, pregnancy/postpartum and pediatric contexts only where rights and review capacity permit. At least 20 should have two or more meaningful decision times; include negative/benign and serious outcomes, not only dramatic diagnoses. Prefer episodes with discordant or changing evidence, comorbidity/polypharmacy, uncertain differential, practical follow-up and a clearly documented outcome. Avoid rare combinations that make a person identifiable unless an expert risk assessment explicitly clears them. Keep every document from the same person/episode in one linkage group; do not split variants or related encounters across development/calibration/hidden partitions.
>
> For every source, supply signed rights/permission evidence from the actual rightsholder or healthcare data controller: allowed commercial benchmark use, derivative/adapted case authoring, EN/ZH translation, internal storage, sharing with clinicians, transmission to named external model vendors (or an explicit prohibition), publication of prompts/labels and summaries, geographic scope, retention/deletion terms, attribution, revocation/expiry and restrictions. For clinical data, supply documented privacy/de-identification approval and permitted transfer/processing route. For exam-bank assets, explicitly identify the rightsholder's authorization; a reseller invoice or ordinary user subscription alone is not enough.
>
> Deliver structured, UTF-8, machine-readable data rather than PDF-only scans. Each de-identified episode must include a pseudonymous `episode_id`; source/rights ID; care setting and jurisdiction; age band/sex only when clinically needed; relative timeline; presenting symptoms and relevant negatives; vitals; comorbidities; allergies; medication name, strength, route, schedule and changes; tests with numeric value, original unit, reference range and collection-relative time; imaging/pathology interpretations; interventions; outcome/follow-up; and evidence confidence or missingness. Preserve original clinical units and add normalized units without overwriting originals. Keep exact date/time and identity crosswalks only in the source controller's restricted environment; use relative hours/days in the export. OCR text must be linked to document/page and manually checked for every fact used in a label. Embedded images require separate identifier review.
>
> For each proposed benchmark **decision point**, deliver: facts available *at that time* (`prompt_facts`); facts deliberately withheld until the next turn (`later_facts`); intended user role and requested action; a neutral clinical construct; at least one safe acceptable answer range, observable `critical_requirements`, prohibited `critical_errors` with proposed severity, reasonable alternative diagnoses/actions, uncertainty and jurisdiction limits, and a source-to-each-requirement mapping. Keep later diagnosis, pathology and actual outcome out of the candidate prompt. The care actually delivered is evidence about the episode, **not automatically the correct answer**. Attach independent current guidance/drug labels for the proposed action and mark every label `draft` until qualified clinical, bilingual and methods review. Produce EN and zh-Hans variants with a meaning-equivalence review record; do not auto-translate clinical Gold.

## Delivery layout and minimum columns

```text
delivery/
  rights_manifest.csv          # one row per licensed source/collection
  episodes.jsonl               # one row per distinct de-identified episode
  observations.csv             # one row per lab/vital/measurement
  medications.csv              # one row per medication event
  document_index.csv           # episode_id, document_id, type, relative_time, page_count, OCR_QA
  decision_points.jsonl        # one row per proposed benchmark decision point and language
  answer_labels.jsonl          # reference behavior, requirements, errors, acceptable alternatives
  evidence_sources.csv         # each independent guideline/drug label, version, locale and rights
  source_to_label.csv          # decision_point_id, requirement_id, supporting source/page/section, limit
  review_status.csv            # de-id, rights, OCR, clinical, language, methods gates and reviewer IDs
  deidentification_report.pdf  # method, residual-risk review, approval and restrictions; no identity crosswalk
  restricted_source_docs/      # only via approved secure transfer; never Git or routine model APIs
```

`rights_manifest.csv` minimum: `asset_id,source_title,version,original_rightsholder,grantor,contract_id,proof_location,commercial_use,derivative_cases,translation,model_api_processing,prompt_publication,label_publication,geography,expiry,attribution,restrictions,approval_status`. Use explicit `yes/no/unknown`; unknown means intake hold. `episodes.jsonl` minimum: `episode_id,source_asset_id,linkage_group_id,jurisdiction,setting,age_band,sex_if_relevant,timeline,outcome_summary,privacy_status`. `decision_points.jsonl` minimum: `decision_point_id,episode_id,relative_time,role,language,category,prompt_facts,later_facts,status`. `answer_labels.jsonl` minimum: `decision_point_id,language,reference_behavior,critical_requirements,critical_errors,acceptable_alternatives,uncertainties,jurisdiction_limits,source_ids,review_status`; keep it separate from the candidate-visible decision point. `evidence_sources.csv` minimum: `source_id,publisher,title,version,publication_date,access_date,stable_url_or_doi,jurisdiction,license_or_access_terms,applicable_population,limitations`. Use stable IDs, Unicode UTF-8, ISO 8601 only for non-patient publication/evidence dates, explicit nulls, and a data dictionary. CSV should be RFC 4180-compatible, with no merged cells or Excel formulas. FHIR R4 `Observation`/`DiagnosticReport` bundles are welcome as an *additional* source export, not a substitute for the decision-point/label package.

**Acceptance gate:** one pilot episode is usable for internal authoring only after rights and privacy approvals, linkage/deduplication, OCR QA, decision-time leakage check, and source-to-label completeness. It remains a `draft` author case until qualified review. Do not load the Xi'an raw scans into `data/alpha/` or call any external model with them. The existing `schemas/case.schema.json` and `schemas/multiturn-pilot.schema.json` define our downstream case shape; the intake files above preserve richer source material before conversion.

Rights/privacy references checked 2026-10-22: [UWorld terms](https://www.uworld.com/terms_conditions.aspx); [China Personal Information Protection Law](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2021-08/20/c_1631050028355286.htm); [HHS de-identification guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/de-identification/index.html) (US guidance, not a substitute for China-law review); [HL7 FHIR R4 DiagnosticReport](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/diagnosticreport.html). This is operational intake guidance, not a legal or clinical approval.
