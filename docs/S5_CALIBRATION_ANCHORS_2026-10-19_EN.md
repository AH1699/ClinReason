# S5 prospective calibration anchor packet — 2026-10-19

The six source-linked patient two-turn calibration **author drafts** now have provisional [criterion anchors](../data/calibration/criterion_anchors_v1.json) and [bilingual answer contrasts](../data/calibration/response_contrasts_v1.json). Each of the 12 exact case-language revisions is bound by a content hash. Every required behavior has observable `met`, `partial`, `missed` and acceptable-equivalent wording; `unclear` is an explicit review state. Each language also has one common safe initial answer and three alternative update answers intended to be strong, safe-but-incomplete, and unsafe. That is **12 initial answer examples plus 36 update examples**, not 48 graded observations. The contrast's partial-target index names its intended primary omission; other omissions may also be present.

These examples are **author hypotheses, not independently adjudicated ground truth**. We did not call a candidate or a judge, assign numerical scores, change the rubric, or promote any case. The expected `strong > partial > unsafe` ordering must be checked later against qualified human labels, particularly where an allegedly partial answer might itself be unsafe. The English-language anchor descriptions are bound to both English and Chinese case hashes but are not proof of Chinese semantic equivalence. Chinese answer examples also require qualified language and clinical review.

The offline [calibration validator](../benchmark/calibration_drafts.py) checks complete family/language coverage, exact case-hash binding, criterion count, distinct response examples, a valid targeted requirement index and matching bilingual critical-error ID. The [portfolio audit](../benchmark/portfolio_audit.py) also verifies canonical anchor/contrast hashes and keeps the six cases out of default development. These checks do **not** judge clinical correctness, translation, the actual quality order, or semantic overlap with the 104 public families. Approved calibration and hidden Gold remain **0**; the single local Git-ignored hidden draft remains separate and was not inspected or scored for this packet.

The anchor boundaries draw on each case's [provenance ledger](../data/calibration/provenance_batch_b.json) and the earlier [valproate ledger](../data/calibration/provenance_v1.json), but the sources do not certify the exact answer text or severity. The [living reviewer handoff](CLINICAL_REVIEW_HANDOFF_EN.md) lists H41 and H43–H48 for case- and answer-level adjudication. Before any judge calibration, reviewers should mark each requirement and response example as acceptable, revise, or reject; settle the unsafe-error thresholds; check both languages independently; and bind decisions to the exact hashes. Only then freeze the packet and use blinded Gemini/Grok judging to measure criterion misses, unsafe false passes, ceiling rates, disagreement and EN/ZH gaps. If judges disagree with reviewed labels, investigate the item or judge rather than changing scores to force the owner's desired lower/mid/premium bands (~60–70/~80/~90).

[Official OpenAI evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices) recommends human feedback to calibrate automated metrics, and its [grader guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/graders) recommends varied good/fair/poor answers with ground-truth grades. We used that as process guidance; no OpenAI-hosted evaluation service was used.
