## Scoring: keep provisional scores separate from comparison readiness

The [S25 offline diagnostic](../benchmark/score_reporting_s25.py) combines independently audited but **non-pooled** S19, S22, S23 and S24 evidence. The [versioned reporting proposal](../config/score_reporting_s25_v1.json) asks for a complete frozen panel, stable exact-packet pass classification, independently approved clinical/bilingual labels and methods release review before a result is marked comparison-ready. This is a proposed reporting layer, **not** an enforced runner rule or a claim that repeated AI ratings are clinically correct.

S19 repeated exact numeric scores in **15/24** cells and provisional pass flags in **22/24**; two identical-packet `TRIAGE-018` EN/ZH partial judgments flipped fail↔pass. S22 saved **23/24** planned first-stage judgments and did not enter its planned repeat. The diagnostic therefore marks **no S19 cell (0/24) or S22 panel as comparison-ready**; reasons include the unapproved labels and release method, with the two exact-packet flips and S22 incompleteness explicitly visible. The 70-point draft pass line, historic numbers, dimension weights and critical-error caps were not changed. `python3 -m benchmark.score_reporting_s25 --summary` reproduces the aggregate without exposing saved answer text. Do not combine S19 and S22 cells or infer a model's population score from these controls. The desired lower ~60–70 / average ~80 / premium ~90 bands remain unfitted aspirations, not targets for forced calibration.

Next scoring decision: qualified reviewers eventually adjudicate the exact `TRIAGE-018`, `TRIAGE-020` and `MEDSAFE-015` criterion/safety/revision boundaries, followed by a separately frozen prospective panel on expert-reviewed anchors. Until then, the present result supports a **comparison hold**, not a revised numeric rule.
