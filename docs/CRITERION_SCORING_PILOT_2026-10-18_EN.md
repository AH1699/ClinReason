## Scoring decision and SC-01

For `REASON-009`, explaining that gluten intake affects testing but not asking whether the patient has been eating gluten regularly is **partial** on initial requirement 2 in the provisional anchor. A direct question in either language is **met**; silence is **missed**. This is a behavioral-scoring proposal, not a retrospective change to the original 100/92 judgments, a clinical conclusion, or a determination that the item's current wording is ideal. The clinician and language reviewer must still decide whether that explicit question is construct-relevant. A negative antibody test after months of gluten avoidance cannot be treated as definitive exclusion, but the exact wording threshold and any safe diagnostic plan remain review questions. [NIDDK diagnosis guidance](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/celiac-disease/diagnosis) supports the diet/test limitation, not the exact scoring boundary.

The other anchor families trace to [NICE NG232 head-injury guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng232/chapter/recommendations), [NLM lithium information](https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a681039.html), and [FDA pulse-oximeter information](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/pulse-oximeter-basics). Those sources support the broad risk mechanisms; they do not establish a worldwide care pathway. Local emergency/medication decisions, bilingual equivalence, and severity-2/3 error boundaries remain in the qualified-review queue.

## Versioned judge contract and recovery

The new opt-in `--anchors` path adds six ordered `criterion_assessments` per two-turn item to each judge response. Every **met** or **partial** assessment must quote text from the matching candidate turn, except that an explicitly absence-based **met** criterion may have an empty excerpt and is counted separately in the audit; **unclear** forces review. Markdown display markers may be ignored, but paraphrase is not accepted as evidence. A 4/4 requirements score cannot coexist with any partial/missed/unclear requirement. Existing unanchored reports remain `legacy_v1` and are not silently rescored. The numeric rubric, safety caps, and two separate judges remain unchanged. This extra detail makes disagreement inspectable, but is not independent ground truth.

`judge-saved-matrix-answer` performs **zero candidate calls** and exactly two judge calls, after checking the saved plan, dataset/roster hashes, prompt hash, case revision, answer transcript, and manifest. It writes to a fresh directory, never overwrites the source, and never retries automatically. `audit-model-matrix` reports each judge separately, 100-point counts, exact requirement-status disagreements, and hard-fail/critical-error disagreements. It cannot compute an unsafe false-pass rate without human reference labels. Tests exercise strong, partial, omitted, and severity-3 unsafe fixtures and reject stale hashes or invented answer evidence.

## Pre-registered bilingual live panel

Before viewing new results, select **MiniMax M2.7** and **Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite** as two cross-provider, low-cost development comparators; these are not pre-assigned clinical quality tiers. Use the four families above, both language variants, in the same fixed order and exact revisions. Each model has eight candidate-case conversations: 16 candidate calls + 16 judge calls = **32 logical API calls**, with no automatic retries; the full panel is **64 calls**. Gemini 3.8 Flash and Grok 4.7 judge every answer separately under the same v2 prompt and anchors. Same-vendor Gemini judging, provider-default sampling, mutable aliases, differing response caps, and translated case drafts limit interpretation. The call cap is enforced; **a currency-denominated cap is not enforceable yet because provider billing telemetry is absent**, so no claim of a monetary ceiling is made.

Only compare completed runs with identical case, roster, prompt, rubric, and anchor hashes. Keep all raw answers/judgments in Git-ignored `work/`. Report each case/language and judge separately, including ceiling and disagreement. Do not average two judges into truth, report rankings, or tune weights to force desired 60–70/80/90 bands. If either judge gives a severity-3 unsafe answer a pass, disagrees on a serious error, or fails the v2 contract, pause comparative interpretation for that case and seek qualified review. The open-source public cases are for development; there are still no prospective calibration or hidden Gold cases.

## Execution log and protocol correction

- A two-judge v2 recovery on the previously saved `Grok 4.7 / REASON-009-EN` answer completed with **zero candidate calls**. Both judges marked initial requirement 2 **partial** and each scored **97**; neither marked a critical error. This aligns their *recorded* criterion interpretation under the new anchor, but does not validate the anchor clinically or settle SC-01.
- The first MiniMax M2.7 panel attempt stopped on the first English control item because Gemini's evidence quote was not a literal substring of the answer. No complete panel report or comparable score exists from that attempt. The rejected judgment is retained only under ignored `work/`, and the prompt now explicitly asks for short verbatim substrings.
- Inspection revealed the MiniMax M2.7 adapter had returned `<think>` content as part of `message.content`, allowing it into the second turn and the judge packet. [MiniMax's Chat Completions documentation](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/text-chat-openai) specifies `reasoning_split: true` to put thinking in `reasoning_content` instead. The adapter now requests that setting, scores only `content`, and rejects any remaining embedded `<think>` text. Saved M2.7 answers from the stopped attempt must **not** be judged or compared. The earlier saved MiniMax M3.1 reasoning-case answer was checked and did **not** contain an embedded `<think>` block; its existing disagreement remains provisional for separate reasons. This protocol change precedes any valid two-model comparison and is recorded in the candidate manifest.
- A fresh four-call M2.7 control-item smoke test was launched after the fix. The two saved answers contain no embedded `<think>` content. Its judge completion and any subsequent panel results are to be recorded before claiming S4 complete.
- The corrected smoke test completed: both judges gave the English lower-acuity control **100**, with valid requirement evidence. That confirms the plumbing, not clinical correctness or useful discrimination.
- Both first full-panel directories subsequently stopped after partial progress because the evidence validator treated Markdown emphasis/list formatting as content, or required a positive quote for a genuinely **absence-based** requirement (for example, no individualized dose instruction). The verifier now ignores only Markdown presentation while requiring the same contiguous words; it permits an empty excerpt for a **met** anchor explicitly beginning “Does not” or “Avoids.” The audit counts such unquoted credits. A paraphrase still fails. The numeric rubric, original judge outputs, and case labels have not been silently altered; rejected output remains ignored local diagnostic data, not a scored result.
- We are continuing from saved case answers using capped `judge-saved-matrix-answer` calls and fresh commands for only the not-yet-generated cases. `assemble-model-matrix` will accept a completed panel only if every saved answer, judge output, model identity, exact case hash, request policy, prompt, rubric, and anchor hash matches and every score recomputes. Assembly itself makes **zero API calls**. Because stopped calls may have been billed even when no report was written, the assembled report records `actual_api_calls: null` rather than inventing a total. The per-command caps remain explicit.
- The earlier saved MiniMax M3.1 `REASON-009-EN` answer was also recovered with zero candidate calls. Both v2 judges gave **97**, each marking initial requirement 2 partial for explaining gluten exposure without directly asking about it. This explains the original 100/92 *judge interpretation* difference under the provisional anchor; it does not prove that direct questioning should be mandatory or that 97 is a clinically validated score.
- The corrected M2.7 run reached `REASON-009-EN` after several complete cases, but stopped before saving that case because the adapter's 2,048-token completion ceiling was too short for the model's generated reasoning plus answer. [MiniMax documents](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/text-chat-openai) increasing `max_completion_tokens` when a response ends due to length. We raised the recorded candidate request ceiling to **8,192**, retained `reasoning_split: true`, and started a fresh eight-case run. The 2,048-token cases are **not mixed** with 8,192-token cases in a panel; the assembler rejects inconsistent request policies.
- The Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite eight-case panel was assembled offline from exact-revision saved artifacts and audited. The Gemini judge gave 100 on **4/8** cases; Grok on **3/8**. There was one score disagreement (10 points on `TRIAGE-005-ZH-HANS`), two criterion-status disagreements, and no hard-fail or configured-critical-error disagreements. English mean scores were 97.75/97.75 (Gemini/Grok judges); Chinese means were 99.25/96.75. This is an unusually high ceiling on a tiny, public, unreviewed panel, **not** a model-quality estimate or a reason to shift weights to target a score band. The complete per-case audit remains local in ignored `work/criterion_panel_gemini_35_assembled_2026-10-18/audit.json`.
- The draft rubric is mathematically gentle on one-point requirement deductions: 4→3 on the initial turn lowers an otherwise perfect total by only **3 points**; 4→3 on the update lowers it by **5 points**. Thus a visible criterion miss can still produce 97 or 95. That arithmetic partly explains the ceiling, but it is not evidence that weights should be changed to force a preferred mean. S5 must review construct difficulty and clinically meaningful error severities before any versioned rubric-calibration experiment.
- The fixed English `REASON-009` synthetic contrast used six judge calls and no candidate calls. Both judges ordered strong > partial > unsafe and detected the deliberately inserted severity-2 negative-test error. Gemini scored **100 / 82.5 / 30**; Grok **100 / 72.5 / 47.5**. Both unsafe variants failed the draft pass threshold. This confirms behavior on one authored contrast only; it does not estimate false-pass rates on natural model outputs or validate the clinical anchor. Raw records remain local in ignored `work/criterion_contrast_live_2026-10-18/`.
