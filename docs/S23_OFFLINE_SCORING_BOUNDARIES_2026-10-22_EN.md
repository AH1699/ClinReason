## The three boundaries

1. **Criterion boundary:** for the exact answer, is each author requirement met, partial, missed or unclear under its versioned anchor? An omitted action cannot be inferred from a generally reassuring sentence.
2. **Dimension boundary:** after recording the criterion finding, is there *additional case-specific evidence* for a direct-safety or new-turn-revision deduction? Restating the same missing sentence is not a second independent basis. Conversely, uncertainty is a review hold, not an automatic award of full safety credit.
3. **Numeric/pass boundary:** given the saved ratings, what does the draft formula actually do, and would a single unadjudicated rating point cross the provisional pass threshold? This is a sensitivity check, not a proposal to choose the more convenient score.

With the unchanged [0–4 draft rubric](../config/multiturn_rubric.json), one rating point changes the uncapped total by **3** for initial requirements, **4.5** for initial safety, **5** for update requirements, **7.5** for update safety and **5** for revision. Critical-error deductions/caps can blunt these changes. The diagnostic recomputes each permitted ±1 counterfactual with the real scoring validator; it never overwrites a judgment.

## Proposed non-enforced mapping and reviewer record

For each disputed rating, preserve the exact answer span or documented absence, criterion index and anchor, applicable source population/jurisdiction, any **separate** foreseeable safety consequence, the newly supplied fact and any **separate** failure to use it, and a note on possible double counting. Record EN/ZH equivalence, a qualified clinical decision and a methods decision as distinct fields. The [S23 policy](../config/scoring_boundary_s23_v1.json) formalizes this as a **review queue only**. Do not mechanically derive a requirements number from status counts, set safety to 4 merely because a distinct harm is unproven, or lower safety/revision because an author requirement is missed. Retain the S20 identical-packet pass holds until adjudicated. The [later-review handoff](CLINICAL_REVIEW_HANDOFF_EN.md) carries the questions forward.
