# S16 PubMed case-report intake pilot — 2026-10-21

This is a **source-intake pilot**, not an expansion of the scored case database. The [citation-only ledger](../data/evidence/pubmed_case_intake_s16_v1.json) records three CC BY case-report candidates and one rights-excluded example. The [structural auditor](../benchmark/pubmed_case_intake.py) checks distinct PMID/PMCID/DOI identities, source/license links, empty derived-case lists and the noncommercial/no-derivatives exclusion. It cannot independently determine legal rights, consent, retractions, medical validity or language equivalence. No article narrative, figure, patient data, candidate prompt or reference answer was imported.
